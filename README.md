# Amazon-Review-Text-Analysis-NLP

NLP project on Amazon product reviews using Python & Colab. Key techniques: Text Cleaning, Tokenization, Lemmatization, Word Cloud, and TF-IDF analysis.

Completed an individual project to analyze customer feedback in a real-world Amazon dataset using Python and Google Colab, to understand what customers focus on in positive and negative reviews.

The project involved sampling, cleaning, and preprocessing 1,465 customer reviews. I performed text normalization, punctuation and stopword removal, tokenization, lemmatization, word frequency analysis, and word cloud visualization to identify the most common topics in customer feedback.

I created sentiment labels from product ratings (1,110 positive and 355 negative reviews) and applied TF-IDF feature extraction (1,000 terms) to compare the most important words in each group. Positive reviews focused on satisfaction aspects such as ease of use and quality, while negative reviews highlighted concerns related to price, product issues, and usage problems.

The analysis also revealed that words like "good" and "nice" appear in negative reviews as well, often within contrasting statements such as "the product is good, but it has problems", showing that word frequency alone does not fully capture customer sentiment.

Technologies & Libraries: Python, Pandas, NumPy, NLTK, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib, WordCloud, Google Colab, Kaggle.
